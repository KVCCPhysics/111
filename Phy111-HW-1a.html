Problem 1.A.3

Tectonic plates are large segments of the Earth’s crust that move slowly. Suppose that one such plate has an average speed of 4.0 cm/year.

  1. What distance does it move in 1 s at this speed? [\(1.3 \times 10^{-9}~m\)]
  2. What is its speed in kilometers per million years? [\(40~km/Myr\)]
