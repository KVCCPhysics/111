Physics 111 - Practice Questions for Exam 1

Problem 1

A spring loaded launcher (similar to the lab launcher) fires a metal ball vertically, and falls back down to its original launch point, hitting the launcher \(t=1.25~s\) later.

  1. Draw a picture of the motion, create a coordinate system and set your origin on the drawing.
  2. Calculate the velocity of the ball when exiting the launcher in meters per second.
  3. In a separate experiment, the same launcher is rotated to launch horizontally off of a \(1.12~m\) tall table. Draw a new picture of the motion and calculate the range the ball travels in this new shot.

Problem 2

A cannon is fired at a mountain to safely trigger an avalanche. The cannon is launched at an angle of \(32^\circ\) above the horizontal, and hits the mountain at its maximum height at a time \(t=2.1~s\) after the shot. The cannon hits the mountain \(975~m\) down range (horizontal).

  1. Draw a picture of the motion, create a coordinate system and set your origin on the drawing.
  2. Calculate the time it will take for the projectile to hit the side of the mountain.
  3. Determine the angle which the cannon is initially launched to hit the mountain.
  4. Calculate the height at which the projectile hits the mountain.

Problem 3

A golfer starts at the tees of a dog-leg par four. The hole is located 112 meters North and 321 meters East. First the golfer hits the ball directly north for 234 yards, followed by a second shot of 123 yards at an angle of 31 degrees North of East. Conversion: 1 yard = 0.9144 meters

  1. Carefully draw a picture of the shots (attempt to draw it to scale).
  2. What is the final displacement vector between the tees and the ball after the second shot (in meters) in Cartesian coordinates?
  3. How far is the ball from the hole after the second shot?
  4. Which cardinal direction would you hit the ball to shoot directly at the hole?
